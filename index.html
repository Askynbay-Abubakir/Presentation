Scope & limits

What can the data
actually tell me?

CAN TEST

Regional association

Whether higher-income regions tend to have higher turnout.

CANNOT TEST

Individual behavior

Whether a specific higher-income person is more likely to vote.

CANNOT PROVE

The mechanism

Whether lower voting costs are actually causing the observed relationship.

DESIGN LIMIT

The number of regional units may limit statistical power and generalizability.
